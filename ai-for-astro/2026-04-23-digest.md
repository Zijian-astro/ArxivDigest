## 3. H-Sets: Hessian-Guided Discovery of Set-Level Feature Interactions in Image Classifiers

- arXiv ID: `2604.22045v1`
- Authors: Ayushi Mehrotra, Dipkamal Bhusal, Michael Clifford, Nidhi Rastogi
- Score: 8
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22045v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.22045v1
- Subjects: Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (cs.CV); Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI)
- Reason: Introduces Hessian-guided feature interaction discovery and set-level attribution for image classifiers, which could transfer to interpreting astronomical image models (e.g., JWST morphology or anomaly detection).

Short digest:
How can we discover and attribute higher-order feature interactions in image classifiers? H-Sets uses input Hessians to detect interacting pixel pairs and merges them into coherent sets, then attributes each set with a set-level gradient method. This yields sparser, more faithful saliency maps that could help interpret deep learning models applied to astronomical imaging.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check whether the Hessian detection stage is computationally feasible for large astronomical images (e.g., JWST mosaics).
- Look for examples or discussion of applying the method to domain-specific image classifiers beyond natural images.
- Examine how the segmentation prior (SAM or other) affects results on images with diffuse or noisy structures typical of astronomy.
