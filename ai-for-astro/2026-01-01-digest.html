Title: When Small Models Are Right for Wrong Reasons: Process Verification for Trustworthy Agents
Authors: Laksh Advani
arXiv ID: 2601.00513v1

Reason: Directly addresses reasoning integrity in LLM agents, which is critical for trustworthy scientific workflows and automated research agents.
Short digest: This paper asks whether small language models (7-9B parameters) can be trusted when they give correct answers, revealing that 50-69% of correct answers contain flawed reasoning. The authors introduce a Reasoning Integrity Score (RIS) to measure process quality, finding that retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) improves reasoning integrity while self-critique harms it.
