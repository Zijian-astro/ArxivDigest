Short digest: This paper asks how to make large language models more interpretable by tracing their outputs back to specific training examples. The authors propose PRISM, a new architecture where each prediction is formed by a sparse, non-negative mixture of learned 'prototypes,' each anchored to a coherent neighborhood of training examples.
