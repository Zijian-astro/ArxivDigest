Title: Executable verification through formalized expert reasoning in astronomical spectroscopy
Authors: Haosong Wang , Ting Tan , Ji Yao , Jiajun Zhang , Qian Zheng , Christophe Yeche , Jean-Paul Kneib , Huanyuan Shan
arXiv ID: 2607.06128
Score: 8
Reason: This paper introduces FORMA, an executable verification protocol for astronomical spectroscopy that uses formalized expert reasoning, which is directly relevant to AI/automated classification and spectral inference for astronomy.
Short digest: The central question is how to automate scientific verification in astronomical spectroscopy beyond prediction. The main result is FORMA, which reconstructs expert reasoning into an auditable workflow that achieves 95.5% agreement with experts on DESI data. This matters for my work because it provides a framework for automated, auditable classification of ambiguous spectra, applicable to JWST high-redshift galaxy and AGN surveys.
Full-text inspection checklist: Check the credibility score threshold and its relation to classification reliability.; Look for examples of spectral evidence extraction and physical audit steps.; Examine how the method handles ambiguous cases and compares to expert adjudication.

Title: Exploring Image-Text Alignment for Radio Galaxy Morphologies
Authors: Erica Lastufka , Mariia Drozdova , Svyatoslav Volosynovskiy
arXiv ID: 2607.06305
Score: 7
Reason: This paper explores image-text alignment for radio galaxy morphologies using vision-language models, which is directly relevant to AI-for-astronomy and automated classification.
Short digest: The central question is whether text captions can capture the same morphological information as radio galaxy images. The main result is that caption-based classification of FR-I/FR-II galaxies performs similarly to images. This matters for my work because it demonstrates the use of multimodal AI for galaxy classification, which could be extended to JWST data for high-redshift galaxies and AGN.
Full-text inspection checklist: Check the classification performance metrics and comparison between image-only and caption-only models.; Look for details on the domain-specific prompt and LoRA fine-tuning approach.; Examine the dataset (MiraBest) and whether the method could generalize to other morphological classes.
