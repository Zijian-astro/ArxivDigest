## 3. Gas-Phase Metallicity and Nitrogen Abundances in Low-Mass Galaxies Down to $M_\star\simeq10^{5.7}\,M_\odot$ at $z\simeq4.5$--$10.1$ from JWST Lensing Cluster Surveys

- arXiv ID: `2607.15515`
- Authors: Hiroya Umeda , Masami Ouchi , Kimihiko Nakajima , Kuria Watanabe , Yuichi Harikane , Yuki Isobe , Moka Nishigaki , Ono Yoshiaki , Hidenobu Yajima , Yi Xu
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15515
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.15515
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies JWST/NIRSpec spectra of high-redshift (z=4.5-10.1) low-mass galaxies, including metallicity and nitrogen abundances, which is highly relevant to galaxy/AGN co-evolution and chemical enrichment at cosmic dawn.

Short digest:
This paper asks how gas-phase metallicity and nitrogen abundances evolve in low-mass galaxies at z~4.5-10.1. Using JWST/NIRSpec spectra from lensing-cluster surveys, they derive new metallicity calibrations and trace the mass-metallicity relation down to M*~10^6.6 Msun, finding a steeper low-mass slope than local. This matters for my work because it provides crucial chemical enrichment constraints for the faint galaxy population that hosts early black holes and little red dots.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the new empirical strong-line metallicity calibrations and how they compare to direct-method measurements.
- Look for the N/O abundance analysis from UV vs. optical lines and the interpretation of the N-rich component.
- Examine the He II 4686/Hbeta ratio and the discussion of WR stars as a nitrogen source.
