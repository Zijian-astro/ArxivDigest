## 3. Tracing the Evolution of the Balmer Break from Cosmic Dawn to Cosmic Noon with JWST

- arXiv ID: `2607.07698`
- Authors: Adarsh Kuruvanthodi , Daniel Schaerer , Rui Marques-Chaves , Andrea Weibel , Damien Korber , Corinne Charbonnel
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07698
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.07698
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies Balmer break evolution with JWST NIRCam from CEERS, JADES, FRESCO, PRIMER, covering high-z galaxies and LRDs.

Short digest:
The central question is how Balmer break strength evolves from z=3.5 to 10 and its correlation with galaxy properties. The main result is that median BB strength increases from 1.1 to 1.5 from cosmic dawn to cosmic noon, driven by stellar age, with extreme BB objects linked to LRDs. This matters for my work as it provides a systematic photometric characterization of BB strength in high-z galaxies and LRDs, aiding SED fitting and source classification.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Look for the median Balmer break strength values and their redshift bins.
- Check the SED-fitting diagnostics from CIGALE, especially for LRDs and objects with extreme BB.
- Examine the correlation plots between BB strength and stellar mass, age, extinction.
- Verify how line contamination is excluded in the photometric BB estimates.
