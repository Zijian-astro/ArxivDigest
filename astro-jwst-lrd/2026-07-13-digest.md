- Reason: Directly studies JWST/NIRSpec IFU observations of Little Red Dots at z~5, focusing on broad-line AGN and host galaxy decomposition, which is central to my research interests.

Short digest:
The central question is whether LRDs consist of a compact central engine (AGN) embedded in a host galaxy. The main result is that spatial decomposition reveals red broad-line cores and blue narrow-line host galaxies, supporting the two-component model. This matters for my work as it provides direct evidence for AGN-host co-evolution in high-redshift LRDs.

- Check the decomposition of broad and narrow Balmer emission lines
- Examine the continuum color maps separating red core and blue host
- Verify the sample selection and IFU data reduction details
