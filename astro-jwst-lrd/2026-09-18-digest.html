Title: Little Red Dots as a Transient Phase of Self-Interacting Dark Matter Assisted Black Hole Growth
Authors: Yu Rong , Shuang-Nan Zhang , Jian-Min Wang , Junxian Wang , Zhicheng He
arXiv ID: 2609.20021
Score: 9
Reason: This paper directly addresses little red dots (LRDs) as a transient phase of black-hole growth, which is central to my research on LRDs, early black-hole growth, and galaxy/AGN co-evolution at cosmic dawn.
Short digest: The paper asks what physical mechanism produces the compact, red, broad-line LRDs seen by JWST. It proposes that LRDs arise from a transient episode of self-interacting dark matter (SIDM)-assisted black-hole growth, where gas inflows create a compact nuclear thick disk and enhanced SIDM density drives rapid black-hole mass growth and obscuration. This matters for my work because it offers a unified model linking LRD morphology, red continua, V-shaped SEDs, X-ray weakness, and finite duty cycles to early black-hole assembly.
Full-text inspection checklist: Look for the predicted LRD duty cycle, luminosity function, and host-galaxy properties that could be compared with JWST surveys.; Check whether the model makes specific predictions for broad emission lines, X-ray weakness, or SED shape that can be tested with NIRSpec and MIRI.; Look for how the SIDM-assisted growth connects to the overmassive black-hole problem and whether it can be distinguished from super-Eddington or phenomenological obscuration models.

Title: Rapid Dark Growth of Seed Black Holes in Self-Interacting Dark Matter
Authors: Yu Rong , Shuang-Nan Zhang , Jian-Min Wang , Junxian Wang
arXiv ID: 2609.20022
Score: 8
Reason: This paper proposes rapid dark growth of seed black holes in self-interacting dark matter, which is directly relevant to early black-hole growth and the LRD phenomenon at cosmic dawn.
Short digest: The paper asks whether SIDM can enable rapid growth of seed black holes in the early Universe. It shows that gas inflow can contract an SIDM halo into a dense reservoir, allowing a seed black hole to gain several orders of magnitude in mass within less than 1 Myr before transitioning to slower accretion. This matters for my work because it provides a physical pathway for producing the massive black holes inferred from JWST LRD and high-redshift AGN observations.
Full-text inspection checklist: Look for the parameter space of halo mass, seed mass, and gas fraction that produces rapid growth and whether it matches LRD host constraints.; Check whether the model predicts observable signatures such as X-ray weakness, broad lines, or compact morphologies.; Look for how the dark-growth phase ends and whether the transition to ordinary AGN is consistent with observed LRD number densities.
