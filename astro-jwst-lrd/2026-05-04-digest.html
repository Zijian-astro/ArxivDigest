Title: Life After the Quasar: Overmassive Black Holes and Remnant Ionised Bubbles in and Around Two z~6.6 Galaxies
Authors: Romain A. Meyer , Pascal A. Oesch , Callum Witten , Richard S. Elllis , Sarah E. I. Bosman , Fred Davies , Alyssa B. Drake , Nicolas Laporte , Jorryt Matthee , Fabian Walter
arXiv ID: 2605.00763
Score: 10
Reason: Directly studies JWST/NIRSpec observations of overmassive black holes in high-redshift galaxies at z~6.6, with broad-line AGN and ionised bubbles, highly relevant to early black-hole growth and galaxy/AGN co-evolution.
Short digest: Central question: How do overmassive black holes in z~6.6 galaxies relate to their hosts and reionization? Main result: Two galaxies host SMBHs with masses ~2e8 Msun and BH-to-stellar-mass ratios 400-800x local, with young stellar populations and double-peaked Lyman-alpha profiles indicating recent quasar activity. Why it matters: Provides direct evidence for episodic quasar activity and overmassive black holes in the Epoch of Reionization, informing models of early SMBH growth and AGN feedback.
Full-text inspection checklist: Look for NIRSpec spectra showing broad Balmer lines and double-peaked Lyman-alpha profiles; Check stellar population modeling results (ages, masses) and BH mass estimates; Examine the ionised bubble size constraints from Lyman-alpha profile fitting
