This is highly relevant for my work on high-z AGN, broad-line/obscured black-hole growth, and galaxy/AGN co-evolution at cosmic dawn.

- Inspect the evidence for companion galaxies or extended ionized gas around J0320-3521 and J1429+5447
- Check the radio-loudness classification and any implications for AGN feedback or environment

## 3. Little Red Dots as a Transient Phase of Self-Interacting Dark Matter Assisted Black Hole Growth

- arXiv ID: `2609.20021`
- Authors: Yu Rong , Shuang-Nan Zhang , Jian-Min Wang , Junxian Wang , Zhicheng He
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20021
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20021
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper directly addresses little red dots (LRDs) as a transient phase of black-hole growth, which is central to my research on LRDs, early black-hole growth, and galaxy/AGN co-evolution at cosmic dawn.

Short digest:
The paper asks what physical mechanism produces the compact, red, broad-line LRDs seen by JWST. It proposes that LRDs arise from a transient episode of self-interacting dark matter (SIDM)-assisted black-hole growth, where gas inflows create a compact nuclear thick disk and enhanced SIDM density drives rapid black-hole mass growth and obscuration. This matters for my work because it offers a unified model linking LRD morphology, red continua, V-shaped SEDs, X-ray weakness, and finite duty cycles to early black-hole assembly.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Look for the predicted LRD duty cycle, luminosity function, and host-galaxy properties that could be compared with JWST surveys.
- Check whether the model makes specific predictions for broad emission lines, X-ray weakness, or SED shape that can be tested with NIRSpec and MIRI.
- Look for how the SIDM-assisted growth connects to the overmassive black-hole problem and whether it can be distinguished from super-Eddington or phenomenological obscuration models.

## 4. A model to mini-quench early galaxies by balancing stellar feedback and gas accretion

- Authors: Judah Luberto , Steven R. Furlanetto
- Score: 8
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19265
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.19265
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper directly addresses JWST-discovered low-mass early galaxies with low star formation, relevant to high-redshift galaxy formation and quenching at cosmic dawn.

Short digest:
The paper asks what causes the mini-quenching observed in low-mass early JWST galaxies. It proposes a model where stellar feedback drives an expanding gas shell that blocks accretion, reproducing observed quenching timescales for plausible parameters, though low-mass halos remain challenging. This matters for interpreting JWST high-z galaxy star formation histories and the role of feedback in early galaxy evolution.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Look for comparisons to observed JWST mini-quenched galaxy stellar masses and timescales
- Check the assumptions about gas accretion geometry and filamentary inflow
- Check whether predicted quenching fractions can be tested with JWST surveys
- Look for any discussion of implications for reionization-era galaxy populations

## 5. Rapid Dark Growth of Seed Black Holes in Self-Interacting Dark Matter

- arXiv ID: `2609.20022`
- Authors: Yu Rong , Shuang-Nan Zhang , Jian-Min Wang , Junxian Wang
- Score: 8
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20022
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20022
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper proposes rapid dark growth of seed black holes in self-interacting dark matter, which is directly relevant to early black-hole growth and the LRD phenomenon at cosmic dawn.

Short digest:
The paper asks whether SIDM can enable rapid growth of seed black holes in the early Universe. It shows that gas inflow can contract an SIDM halo into a dense reservoir, allowing a seed black hole to gain several orders of magnitude in mass within less than 1 Myr before transitioning to slower accretion. This matters for my work because it provides a physical pathway for producing the massive black holes inferred from JWST LRD and high-redshift AGN observations.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Look for the parameter space of halo mass, seed mass, and gas fraction that produces rapid growth and whether it matches LRD host constraints.
- Check whether the model predicts observable signatures such as X-ray weakness, broad lines, or compact morphologies.
- Look for how the dark-growth phase ends and whether the transition to ordinary AGN is consistent with observed LRD number densities.

Short digest:
The paper asks how dust attenuation profiles vary radially in main-sequence galaxies at z~1. It finds diverse radial attenuation profiles, with most flat or positive, and a median stellar-to-nebular reddening ratio of 0.88. This matters for interpreting JWST spectra and SEDs of high-redshift galaxies, especially for dust correction and star formation rate estimates.

Full-text inspection checklist:

- Check how single-aperture versus spatially resolved SFRs compare
- Check the Balmer decrement methodology and assumptions
- Look for any discussion of implications for higher-redshift JWST samples
