- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.11084
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: High-resolution ALMA imaging of a z=6.5 gravitationally lensed quasar, directly relevant to high-redshift AGN, dust heating, and star formation rate measurements.

Short digest:
The paper asks how much AGN heating contributes to dust emission in a high-redshift quasar. It finds AGN dominates at r<100 pc and contributes ~13% of sub-mm flux, implying star formation rates are mildly overestimated. This matters for accurately measuring SFRs in high-z quasars and understanding AGN-host co-evolution.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Look at the reconstructed source-plane dust continuum map and the size of the compact core (<200 pc).
- Check the radiative transfer model separating AGN-heated vs star-heated dust components.
- Examine the lensing model (SIE+shear) and magnification factor (4.63).
- Verify the AGN heating fraction (~13%) and implications for SFR overestimation.
