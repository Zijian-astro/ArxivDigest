## 1. Compact Core, Extended Reach: A Bipolar kpc-Scale Elongation in a Little Red Dot at $z \approx 5.5$

- arXiv ID: `2606.18342`
- Authors: Zhiyuan Ji , Yang Sun , Mauro Giavalisco , Yongda Zhu , George H. Rieke , Christina C. Williams , Michael V. Maseda , Jianwei Lyu , Marcia Rieke , Sandro Tacchella
- Score: 10
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18342
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.18342
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies a Little Red Dot at z~5.5 with JWST/NIRCam and VLT/MUSE, highly relevant to LRDs, AGN, and galaxy/AGN co-evolution.

Short digest:
Central question: What is the internal structure and nature of the Little Red Dot LRD-204851 at z=5.482? Main result: LRD-204851 shows a bipolar kpc-scale elongation in UV continuum and emission lines, with a double-peaked Lyα profile and tentative N V detection. Why it matters: Provides direct evidence of a central engine's impact on its host galaxy on kpc scales, crucial for understanding LRD nature and AGN feedback at high redshift.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Examine the [O III] and Hα emission-line maps for bipolar structure and clumps.
- Check the Lyα profile modeling for biconical outflow geometry.
- Look for N V λ1238 detection and its velocity alignment with the bipolar structure.

- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18334
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.18334
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies JWST-observed high-redshift galaxies with nitrogen enhancement, relevant to galaxy chemical evolution and AGN contamination.

Short digest:
Central question: What drives the strong nitrogen excess in early JWST galaxies? Main result: N/O-enhanced galaxies at z~4-8.5 are caught briefly after starbursts, with Wolf-Rayet or AGB winds driving nitrogen enrichment. Why it matters: Provides insight into chemical enrichment and bursty star formation in high-redshift galaxies, relevant to understanding galaxy/AGN co-evolution.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check stacked spectra for Wolf-Rayet signatures and Balmer jumps.
- Look for outflow signatures in [O III] and Hα line profiles.
- Examine the N/O enhancement fraction as a function of redshift.
