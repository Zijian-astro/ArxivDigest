- Reason: Directly studies molecular gas in dusty star-forming galaxies in a protocluster at z=2.16 using ALMA, relevant to high-redshift galaxy evolution and AGN.

This informs galaxy evolution in dense environments at cosmic noon, relevant to my high-redshift work.

Short digest:
Can the Ne23 mid-IR line ratio reliably measure metallicity? The authors empirically calibrate O/H-Ne23 using JWST/MIRI MRS observations of H II regions and low-metallicity galaxies. ## 7. Active Galactic Nucleus Feedback in an Elliptical Galaxy. IV. The Importance of the Jet Wind Coupling

- Authors: Minhang Guo , Feng Yuan , Suoqing Ji , Bocheng Zhu
- Score: 4
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27332
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.27332
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; High Energy Astrophysical Phenomena (astro-ph.HE)
- Reason: Simulation-based AGN feedback study, but focused on elliptical galaxies at low redshift, not high-z JWST observations.

Short digest:
This paper asks how jet and wind feedback from an AGN couple to suppress star formation in an elliptical galaxy. It finds that jet-wind coupling via Kelvin-Helmholtz instability produces stronger star-formation suppression than either mechanism alone. The detailed feedback physics is relevant for understanding AGN host-galaxy co-evolution, but the low-redshift, simulation-only focus limits direct applicability to my high-z JWST work.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check whether the simulation includes any high-redshift or early-universe initial conditions.
- Look for quantitative comparisons of feedback efficiency that could be scaled to high-z systems.
