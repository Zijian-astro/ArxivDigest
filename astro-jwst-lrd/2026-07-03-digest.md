## 2. Radiative filtering unifies broad-line phenomenology in active galactic nuclei

- arXiv ID: `2607.01479`
- Authors: Mohammad Hassan Naddaf
- Score: 8
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01479
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.01479
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; High Energy Astrophysical Phenomena (astro-ph.HE); Instrumentation and Methods for Astrophysics (astro-ph.IM)
- Reason: Unifies broad-line phenomenology in AGN across accretion regimes, directly relevant to broad-line AGN and weak-line quasars at high redshift.

Short digest:
Central question: Why do broad emission lines weaken or disappear in both very low- and very high-accretion AGN? Main result: Line formation is governed by the filtered ionizing radiation field, not intrinsic luminosity, creating a finite window for BEL formation. Why it matters: Provides a unified physical explanation for broad-line absence in weak-line quasars and low-luminosity AGN, relevant to interpreting high-redshift AGN spectra and LRD classification.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the quantitative model predictions for BEL strength vs accretion rate and black-hole mass.
- Look for the reproduction of the Baldwin effect and R_Fe trend.
- Examine the breakdown of standard BLR scaling relations in extreme regimes.
