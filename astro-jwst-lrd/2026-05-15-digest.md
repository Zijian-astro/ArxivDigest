- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.13967
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; Cosmology and Nongalactic Astrophysics (astro-ph.CO)
- Reason: This paper uses JWST observations of bursty star formation at z~4-6 to predict enhanced shot-noise in line-intensity mapping, directly relevant to high-redshift galaxy formation and JWST-era science.

Short digest:
The central question is how JWST-observed bursty star formation affects line-intensity mapping power spectra. The main result is that burstiness boosts shot noise by factors of ~2-7 for H-alpha and other lines at z~6. This matters because it transforms detectability and cosmological applications of LIM, and opens a new diagnostic of high-redshift star-formation physics.

Full-text inspection checklist:

- Look for the mass dependence of the burstiness and its impact on predictions.
- Examine the cross-line shot-noise correlations as a probe of time coherence.
