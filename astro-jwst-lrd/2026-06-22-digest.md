- Score: 8
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19459

- Reason: Directly addresses early reionization with Pop III.1 stars, relevant to high-redshift galaxy and AGN studies at cosmic dawn, including black hole growth.

Short digest:
Central question: Can an early phase of reionization from Pop III.1 stars achieve high tau while remaining consistent with Ly-alpha forest and pkSZ constraints?
