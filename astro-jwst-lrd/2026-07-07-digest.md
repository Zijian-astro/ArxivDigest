Short digest:
Central question: What is the population of dust-obscured AGN at z>5 revealed by JWST/MIRI imaging? Main result: 16 MIRI-selected AGN at z=4.5-7.2 identified, including 12 new, with narrow-line AGN representing obscured population missed by broad-line searches. Why it matters: Establishes MIRI imaging as key for complete census of early SMBH growth, directly relevant to LRD and obscured AGN studies.

Full-text inspection checklist:

- Look for narrow-line AGN classification and obscuration mechanisms
- Examine bolometric luminosity function comparison with broad-line AGN and LRDs
- Check individual source properties for LRD candidates

## 2. Lyman-alpha haloes in the aftermath of reionisation

- arXiv ID: `2607.05087`
- Authors: Daniil Smirnov , Lutz Wisotzki , Tanya Urrutia , John Pharo , Ramona Augustin , Yucheng Guo , Daria Kozlova , Haruka Kusakabe , Jorryt Matthee , Ismael Pessa , Joop Schaye
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05087
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.05087
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies Lyman-alpha haloes around high-redshift galaxies at z>=6, relevant for reionization-era galaxies and Lyα emission.

Short digest:
How do Lyman-alpha haloes evolve around galaxies after reionization? The paper finds LAHs at z>=6 are three times smaller than at z~3, with narrower line widths. This matters for understanding Lyα escape, reionization, and galaxy evolution at high redshift.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check detection fractions and scale lengths of LAHs at z>=6 vs z~3.
- Examine stacked profiles for undetected haloes.
- Look for line width measurements and comparison between samples.
- Assess discussion of reionization-driven vs host-galaxy-driven scenarios.

- Authors: L. Y. Aaron Yung , Rachel S. Somerville , Steven L. Finkelstein , Kartheik G. Iyer
- Score: 8
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02650
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.02650
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies galaxy star formation histories from cosmic dawn to reionization using a semi-analytic model, relevant to JWST-era high-redshift galaxy formation.

Short digest:
Central question: What are the predicted star formation histories of galaxies from z~14 to z~6? Main result: Median SFHs are rapidly rising, but individual SFHs show diverse bursts and mini-quenching, with very young stellar populations at z>12. Why it matters: Provides theoretical context for interpreting JWST observations of early galaxies and their stellar populations.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check SFH diversity plots for different mass bins and redshifts
- Look for t_50 and t_90 values and their dependence on stellar mass and redshift
- Examine synthetic photometry modeling assumptions for young stellar populations

## 4. Physically motivated AGN emissivity profiles and their effects on quasar microlensing signatures. 1. Multi-epoch accretion disc size inference

- arXiv ID: `2607.03291`
- Authors: Scott Hagen (1,2,3), Carina Fian (3,4) ((1) IFPU, (2) SISSA, (3) INAF-OATs, (4) University of Nova Gorica)
- Score: 7
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03291
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.03291
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; High Energy Astrophysical Phenomena (astro-ph.HE)
- Reason: Directly addresses AGN accretion disc size inference with microlensing, relevant to AGN structure and broad-line region contamination.

Short digest:
This paper asks whether diffuse BLR emission biases microlensing-inferred accretion disc sizes. It shows that including a BLR component can explain the disc-size problem by smoothing caustic networks. This matters because it provides a physical explanation for the disc-size discrepancy in AGN, relevant to understanding AGN structure in high-z sources.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the agnsed model and how it predicts emissivity profiles and SEDs.
- Look for the fractional contribution of BLR to the bandpass and its effect on half-light radius.
- Examine mock microlensing light curves showing the bias from composite disc+BLR interpretation.
