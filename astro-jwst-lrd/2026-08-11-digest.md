Short digest:

- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly relevant to AGN feedback and multiphase gas diagnostics with JWST MIRI/MRS, though focused on local AGN rather than high-redshift. Provides framework for interpreting PAH and gas emission in AGN that can inform high-z studies.

Short digest:
This paper asks how radiative and kinetic AGN feedback modes affect the surrounding interstellar medium in nearby AGN. Using JWST MIRI/MRS spatially resolved spectroscopy, it finds that both modes suppress PAH emission and explains bimodal PAH ratios. This matters for calibrating SFR indicators in AGN and for developing diagnostics applicable to high-redshift AGN and LRDs.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check for spatially resolved maps of PAH, H2, and ionized gas line ratios that distinguish radiative vs. kinetic feedback
- Look for quantitative separation of shock vs. irradiation effects, e.g., line-ratio diagnostics or excitation diagrams
- Assess sample selection and whether results can be extrapolated to high-redshift compact red sources
