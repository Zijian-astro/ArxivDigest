Title: How massive and clumpy must a quasar wind be to create emission line blueshifts?

arXiv ID: 2607.25035
Score: 6
Reason: The paper studies quasar wind properties and C IV emission line blueshifts, which is relevant to understanding broad-line AGN and quasar feedback at high redshift.
Short digest: The central question is how much mass, momentum, and energy quasar winds must carry to produce blueshifted C IV emission lines.
