## 1. Where did all the Little Red Dots go? The abundance of LRD analogues among objects with broad lines at $z < 0.35$

- arXiv ID: `2608.05267`
- Authors: Lasse Seyberlich , Sarah E. I. Bosman
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05267
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05267
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper directly addresses Little Red Dots (LRDs) by searching for local analogues, which is highly relevant to my interest in LRDs and their nature.

Short digest:
The central question is whether local analogues of LRDs exist among broad-line objects at low redshift. The main result is that LRDs are extremely rare, with only nine analogues found among SDSS broad-line objects. This matters for my work by providing a local sample to study LRD properties and potentially constrain their physical nature.

Full-text inspection checklist:

- Look for the properties of the nine analogues, especially J0302-0101, and their comparison to high-redshift LRDs.
- Inspect the photometric and spectroscopic data used to identify analogues.

The central question is how emission lines form and broaden in scattering-dominated media relevant to LRDs. The main result is that a broken power-law model can fit H-alpha line profiles in LRDs. ## 3. On the Origin of the Ly$α$ Damping Wing in Galaxies at $8\le z \le 10$: Explorations using the NINJA Simulations

- arXiv ID: `2608.05281`
- Authors: Sukanya Mallik , Raghunathan Srianand , Nishikanta Khandai
- Score: 7
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05281
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05281
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; Cosmology and Nongalactic Astrophysics (astro-ph.CO)
- Reason: This paper uses simulations to interpret Ly-alpha damping wings in high-redshift galaxies, relevant to reionization-era galaxies and JWST observations.

Short digest:
The central question is what contributes to the Ly-alpha damping wing in galaxies at z=8-10. The main result is that the strongest absorbers require contributions from ISM, CGM, and IGM, with unresolved gas in birth clouds. This matters for my work on high-redshift galaxies and reionization, as it helps interpret JWST Ly-alpha observations.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the mock spectra generation and the assumed ionized-bubble models.
- Look for the comparison of inferred NHI distributions with JWST observations.
- Inspect the role of halo mass and stellar mass in the damping wing strength.
