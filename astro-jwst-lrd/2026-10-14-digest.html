Title: A radio counterpart of the local little-red-dot J092359+415736
Authors: Tao An , Ruqiu Lin , Ailing Wang
arXiv ID: 2610.17559
Score: 9
Reason: This paper directly studies a local little red dot with radio observations, connecting LRD phenomenology to accreting black holes and multi-frequency variability. It is highly relevant to my work on LRDs, AGN, and black-hole growth.
Short digest: The paper asks whether the local LRD J092359+415736 has a radio counterpart and what powers it. This matters for my work because it provides multi-frequency, multi-epoch evidence for AGN activity in LRDs, complementing high-redshift JWST studies.

Title: Anisotropic Radiation in Super-Eddington Active Galactic Nuclei: The Responsivity of Broad-Line Regions to Continuum Variability
Authors: Feng-Na Fang , Pu Du , Jian-Min Wang
arXiv ID: 2610.15255
Score: 8
Reason: This paper directly addresses anisotropic ionizing radiation in super-Eddington AGN and explicitly discusses implications for JWST little red dots, connecting BLR reverberation physics to LRD interpretation.
Short digest: The paper asks whether the ionizing radiation incident on broad-line region clouds in super-Eddington AGN is anisotropic relative to the observer's line of sight. It finds that H-beta responsivity in super-Eddington AGN implies ionizing fluxes differing significantly from the line-of-sight continuum, unlike sub-Eddington AGN. This matters for my work because it offers a physical mechanism that could explain the compact broad-line and reddened AGN signatures seen in JWST little red dots.
Full-text inspection checklist: Look for the quantitative comparison between inferred BLR ionizing flux and observed line-of-sight continuum in super- versus sub-Eddington AGN; Check the discussion connecting anisotropic ionizing radiation to JWST little red dots and whether it predicts specific emission-line ratios or continuum shapes; Look for the sample selection and reverberation-lag measurements used to derive H-beta responsivity; Check whether the paper provides diagnostics that could distinguish anisotropic BLR illumination from dust reddening in LRD SEDs

arXiv ID: 2610.17575
Score: 8
Reason: This paper directly addresses galaxy stellar mass growth at z>~5, including the role of mergers versus star formation, which is highly relevant to high-redshift galaxy formation and JWST observations. It also discusses AGN feedback and implications for interpreting JWST pair fractions.
Short digest: The paper asks whether mergers or in-situ star formation dominate stellar mass growth at cosmic dawn.
