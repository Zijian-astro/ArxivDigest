## 1. RUBIES: The Evolution of the Ionization Parameter from 0 < z < 9

- arXiv ID: `2605.30410`
- Authors: Nikko J. Cleri , Zach J. Lewis , Joel Leja , Jakob M. Helton , Emilie Burnham , Olivia Curtis , Anna de Graaff , Michaela Hirschmann , Harley Katz , Michael V. Maseda , Ian McConachie , Adele Plat , Lucie Scharre
- Score: 10
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30410
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.30410
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies ionization parameter evolution at 3<z<9 using JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy from RUBIES, highly relevant to high-z galaxy/AGN conditions.

Short digest:
Central question: How does the ionization parameter U evolve from z=0 to z=9? Main result: U increases with redshift and sSFR, decreases with mass, and increases by ~4x from z=2 to z=6 at fixed mass and sSFR. Why it matters: Provides key constraints on nebular conditions in high-z galaxies, directly applicable to interpreting JWST spectra of LRDs and AGN.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the O32-based U inference methodology and photoionization model assumptions
- Look for systematic uncertainty estimates and the multivariate prediction for U
- Examine the comparison with lower-z samples (SDSS, LEGA-C, KBSS)
