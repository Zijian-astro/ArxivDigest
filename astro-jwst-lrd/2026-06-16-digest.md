- Reason: Directly studies HST-dark galaxies at z>2 using JWST/UNCOVER, deriving stellar masses, SFRs, and dust properties; highly relevant to high-redshift galaxy and AGN research.

Short digest:
The central question is whether JWST can uncover optically invisible (HST-dark) galaxies at cosmic noon and beyond. The main result is that 113 high-z HST-dark galaxies are found, including low-mass dusty systems down to 10^7.5 Msun. This matters because it reveals a previously missing galaxy population, directly informing the high-redshift galaxy census and dust-obscured AGN searches.

Full-text inspection checklist:

- Look for SED fitting results showing stellar mass, dust extinction (Av), and SFR distributions.
- Examine the gravitational magnification corrections and their impact on derived properties.
- Check for any broad-line AGN or LRD candidates within the sample.

## 6. Seeing through the dust: Unraveling near-infrared variability in type 2 active galactic nuclei

- arXiv ID: `2606.15016`
- Authors: J. Cerón-Meneses , P. Arévalo , P. Sánchez-Sáez , W. Yu , P. Lira , B. Milvang-Jensen
- Score: 8
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15016
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.15016
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies NIR variability of type 2 AGNs using UltraVISTA data, relevant to AGN classification and obscured AGN identification in deep extragalactic surveys.

Short digest:
Can NIR variability distinguish between different types of AGNs? The authors find that 7-17% of type 2 AGNs are variable in the NIR, with variability fractions increasing toward Ks, and classify variable sources into disk-dominated, torus-dominated, and highly obscured groups. This provides an independent diagnostic for confirming optical classifications and identifying weak or misclassified type 1 AGNs, directly relevant to AGN selection in JWST fields.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the DRW variability amplitude wavelength dependence plots
- Look for the fraction of X-ray unobscured vs obscured type 2 AGNs that show NIR variability
- Examine the classification of variable sources into disk/torus/highly obscured groups
