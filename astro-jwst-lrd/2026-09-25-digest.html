Title: CERIDWEN: Fast and Flexible GPU-Accelerated Stellar Population Inference
Authors: Amanda Stoffers , Sandro Tacchella , Benjamin D. Johnson
arXiv ID: 2609.30145
Score: 9
Reason: This is a GPU-accelerated SED fitting framework for JWST high-redshift galaxies, directly relevant to my interests in SED fitting, stellar masses, dust attenuation, and Bayesian inference. It is highly relevant to JWST survey analysis and source classification.
Short digest: The paper asks how to make flexible stellar-population inference practical for large JWST and future survey datasets. It presents CERIDWEN, a JAX-based GPU-native SED fitting framework with differentiable forward modeling, non-parametric SFHs, and joint chemical-enrichment inference, achieving ~134x faster fits than CPU Prospector. For my work, this is directly useful for fitting large samples of high-z galaxies and AGN, including LRDs, with more flexible models and calibrated uncertainties.
