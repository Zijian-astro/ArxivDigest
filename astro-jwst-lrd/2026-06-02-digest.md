- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly studies LRDs as DCBH candidates at z>8.5 using simulations, linking UV diversity to BH ages.

## 3. Signatures of Accreting Black Holes in Line Intensity Mapping

- arXiv ID: `2606.01603`
- Authors: Erika Ogata , Kana Moriwaki , Adam Lidz , Rui Lan Jun , Naoki Yoshida
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01603
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.01603
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper directly studies the contribution of accreting black holes to line intensity mapping at high redshift, using IllustrisTNG simulations, which is highly relevant to AGN and high-redshift galaxy studies.

Short digest:
The central question is how accreting black holes contribute to Hα and He II line intensity mapping signals. The main result is that BH contribution reaches 40-80% at cosmic noon and dominates shot noise. This matters because it shows BH accretion is essential for interpreting future LIM surveys like SPHEREx.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Look at the mock intensity maps and power spectra showing BH vs star formation contributions
- Check the voxel intensity distribution (VID) for Hα and He II
- Examine the redshift evolution of BH contribution fraction
- Look for discussion of SPHEREx and CDIM detectability
