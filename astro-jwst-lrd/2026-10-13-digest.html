Score: 10
Reason: This paper presents JWST/NIRSpec and ALMA observations of a dust-reddened quasar at z=6.55, directly matching my interests in high-redshift obscured AGN, black-hole growth, and galaxy/AGN co-evolution. It also addresses SED fitting, extinction, and host-galaxy properties at cosmic dawn.
Short digest: The paper asks what the nuclear and host-galaxy properties are of the dust-reddened quasar J1440+0019 at z=6.55. JWST/NIRSpec reveals an SMC-like extinction law with E(B-V)=0.20, a black-hole mass of log(MBH/Msun)=9.04, Eddington ratio ~0.8, and an ionized outflow, while ALMA shows an extremely [C II]-luminous, dust-rich host. This is highly relevant because it provides a benchmark for identifying and characterizing reddened, rapidly accreting quasars and their hosts during reionization, directly informing my work on high-redshift AGN and galaxy/AGN co-evolution.

Title: An empirical calibration of the spectral resolution of JWST NIRSpec PRISM
Authors: Hang Zhou , Xin Wang , Yuxuan Pang , Shengzhe Wang , Karl Glazebrook , Themiya Nanayakkara , Hu Zhan
arXiv ID: 2610.13492
Score: 9
Reason: This paper empirically calibrates JWST NIRSpec PRISM spectral resolution, directly relevant to my NIRSpec spectroscopy of high-redshift galaxies and AGN. Accurate line-width and resolution knowledge is essential for broad-line and emission-line measurements.
Short digest: The paper asks how the effective spectral resolution of JWST NIRSpec PRISM varies with wavelength and source morphology. Using archival galaxies from z~0.5 to 10 and the [O III] doublet, it finds the empirical resolution exceeds pre-launch JDox values by factors of 1.03-1.63 and provides a wavelength- and morphology-dependent calibration. This matters for my work because it affects how I interpret emission-line widths, broad-line AGN, and kinematic measurements in NIRSpec PRISM spectra of high-redshift sources.
Full-text inspection checklist: Look for the empirical resolving-power curve versus wavelength and how it compares to JDox; Check the multi-Gaussian fitting procedure and FWHM measurement methodology; Examine the source-size/morphology dependence and the root-sum-square formula; Assess whether the calibration is applicable to compact LRDs and broad-line AGN

Title: Exploring Case B I: The Footprints of Hidden Radiation Fields in H I Balmer Decrements

arXiv ID: 2610.12715
Score: 6
Reason: This paper explores departures from Case B Balmer decrements due to hidden radiation fields, directly relevant to interpreting high-redshift emission-line ratios and AGN/nebular diagnostics.
Short digest: The paper asks what additional physics can produce Balmer decrement departures from Case B in high-redshift systems. It shows that a soft non-ionizing radiation field from cool sources can strongly perturb Halpha/Hbeta and Hgamma/Hbeta ratios in a way distinguishable from dust reddening. This matters for my work because Balmer line ratios are key diagnostics for dust attenuation and AGN contamination in high-z galaxy spectra.
