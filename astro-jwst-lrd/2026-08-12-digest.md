## 1. Unveiling and Characterising Ubiquitous Nitrogen Enhancement in $6 \leq z \leq 10$ Galaxies with JWST Spectroscopy

- arXiv ID: `2608.10063`
- Authors: Raunaq Singh Rai , Guido Roberts-Borsani
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10063
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.10063
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: Directly uses JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy to measure N/O and C/O abundances in z=6-10 galaxies, addressing chemical enrichment and star formation in the early universe, highly relevant to high-redshift galaxy evolution.

Short digest:
The central question is whether nitrogen enhancement is common in z=6-10 galaxies. The main result is that a stacking analysis of 135 galaxies shows supersolar N/O and subsolar C/O, explained by delayed AGB enrichment. This matters because it informs chemical enrichment and star-formation histories in the first billion years, relevant to my study of early galaxies.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the stacking methodology and how composite spectra are built
- Look for the direct-T_e abundance derivation and its uncertainties
- Examine the comparison to cosmological simulations and the role of AGB yields
