- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; High Energy Astrophysical Phenomena (astro-ph.HE)
- Reason: Directly studies little red dots (LRDs) at high redshift using JWST/NIRCam imaging and slitless spectroscopy from the EIGER survey, a core topic.

Short digest:
Central question: What are the number densities and properties of little red dots at z~2 compared to their peak at z~5? Main result: LRDs at z~2 have number densities ~7e-6 cMpc^-3, showing a gentle decline from z~5, and constitute <3% of the AGN population. Why it matters: This provides crucial constraints on the evolution of LRDs and their connection to AGN across cosmic time, directly informing my work on high-redshift AGN and compact red sources.

Full-text inspection checklist:

- Examine the HeI/Pa-gamma ratio and Balmer break strength distributions that distinguish LRDs from AGNs.
- Look at the number density evolution plot comparing EIGER results with previous photometric surveys at z~5.
