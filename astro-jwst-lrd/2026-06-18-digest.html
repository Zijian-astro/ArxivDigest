Title: Compact Core, Extended Reach: A Bipolar kpc-Scale Elongation in a Little Red Dot at $z \approx 5.5$
Authors: Zhiyuan Ji , Yang Sun , Mauro Giavalisco , Yongda Zhu , George H. Rieke , Christina C. Williams , Michael V. Maseda , Jianwei Lyu , Marcia Rieke , Sandro Tacchella
arXiv ID: 2606.18342
Score: 10
Reason: Directly studies a Little Red Dot at z~5.5 with JWST/NIRCam and VLT/MUSE, highly relevant to LRDs, AGN, and galaxy/AGN co-evolution.
Short digest: Central question: What is the internal structure and nature of the Little Red Dot LRD-204851 at z=5.482? Main result: LRD-204851 shows a bipolar kpc-scale elongation in UV continuum and emission lines, with a double-peaked Lyα profile and tentative N V detection. Why it matters: Provides direct evidence of a central engine's impact on its host galaxy on kpc scales, crucial for understanding LRD nature and AGN feedback at high redshift.
Full-text inspection checklist: Examine the [O III] and Hα emission-line maps for bipolar structure and clumps.; Check the Lyα profile modeling for biconical outflow geometry.; Look for N V λ1238 detection and its velocity alignment with the bipolar structure.
