- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07832

- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper directly addresses the detection of Population III galaxies using emission line diagnostics, which is highly relevant to high-redshift galaxy formation and JWST observations of early galaxies.

Short digest:
