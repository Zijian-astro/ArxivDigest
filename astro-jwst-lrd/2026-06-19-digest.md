## 1. Reimagining SED Fitting with Cosmological Galaxy Simulations and Machine Learning

- arXiv ID: `2606.19447`
- Authors: Dhruv T. Zimmerman , Desika Narayanan
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19447
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.19447
- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; Instrumentation and Methods for Astrophysics (astro-ph.IM)
- Reason: This paper presents a machine-learning SED fitting tool trained on 3D radiative transfer simulations, directly relevant to my interests in SED fitting, galaxy properties, and AI for astronomy.

Short digest:
This paper asks whether a machine-learning model trained on simulated galaxy photometry can outperform traditional SED fitting. The main result is that Phot-Gal outperforms prospector in recovering redshift, stellar mass, dust mass, and SFR. This matters for my work because it offers a faster, potentially more accurate method for inferring physical properties of high-redshift galaxies and AGN from JWST photometry.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check how Phot-Gal handles AGN contamination or whether it is trained on galaxies without AGN.
- Look for validation on high-redshift (z>4) simulated galaxies to assess applicability to my targets.
- Examine the uncertainty calibration, especially when few photometric bands are available.

## 2. Fireworks at Cosmic Dawn: relieving BAO-CMB tensions with the Pop III.1 Flash

- arXiv ID: `2606.19459`
- Authors: Yash Aggarwal , Christopher Cain , Garett Lopez , Hy Trac , Anson D'Aloisio , Philip Tanedo , Jonathan C. Tan
- Score: 9
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19459
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.19459
- Subjects: Cosmology and Nongalactic Astrophysics (astro-ph.CO) ; Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper directly addresses early reionization, Pop III stars, and CMB constraints, highly relevant to high-redshift galaxy formation and cosmic dawn.

Short digest:
This paper asks whether an early phase of reionization from Pop III.1 stars can achieve a high CMB optical depth while remaining consistent with Ly-alpha forest and pkSZ constraints. The main result is that a fiducial model with tau=0.087 evades 2-sigma pkSZ limits. This matters for my work because it provides a framework for understanding early ionizing sources and their impact on reionization, relevant to high-redshift galaxies and AGN I study.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the predicted number densities and properties of Pop III.1 stars for comparison with JWST observations.
- Look for predictions of ionizing photon escape fractions and their impact on galaxy SEDs.
- Examine how the model's source clustering assumptions affect predictions for high-z galaxy surveys.

- Score: 8

- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.19449
- Subjects: Cosmology and Nongalactic Astrophysics (astro-ph.CO) ; Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA)
- Reason: This paper presents an analytical model for reionization history and photoionization rate, directly relevant to high-redshift galaxy studies and the Epoch of Reionization.

Short digest:
This paper asks whether a self-consistent analytical model can predict both the reionization history and the photoionization rate. The main result is a percent-level accurate model in x_i and 20-30% accurate in Gamma_HI at z<6. This matters for my work because it provides a fast tool to interpret Ly-alpha forest constraints and reionization observables relevant to high-redshift galaxies and AGN.

Full-text inspection checklist:
- Check the model's accuracy at z>6, where my high-redshift targets reside.
- Look for comparisons to JWST-era observations of high-redshift galaxies.
- Examine how the model handles ionizing photon output from galaxies versus AGN.
