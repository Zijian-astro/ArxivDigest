Short digest:
This paper asks whether 'Little Red Dots' at high redshift are dominated by a central engine (Black Hole Star) rather than stellar light. It presents a new photometric selection based on BH* templates that identifies 241 such candidates from JWST imaging across z~1.7-9.3. This matters for your work because it provides a new method to isolate AGN-dominated LRDs and quantifies their number density evolution, directly informing early black-hole growth studies.

- Examine the number density evolution plot (z~5-6 peak) and the comparison to V-shaped LRD samples.
