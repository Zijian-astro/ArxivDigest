Title: A nonsingular, anisotropic universe in a black hole with torsion and particle production
Authors: Nikodem J. Popławski
arXiv ID: 2007.11556
Score: 1
Reason: This paper is a purely theoretical cosmology paper about black hole interiors and torsion, with no connection to JWST observations or high-redshift galaxies/AGN.
Short digest: The central question is whether a universe can form inside a black hole with torsion and avoid a singularity. The main claim is that torsion and particle production can prevent the singularity and cause a nonsingular bounce. This is irrelevant to my work on JWST observations of high-redshift galaxies and AGN.
