- Subjects: Astrophysics of Galaxies (astro-ph.GA) ; High Energy Astrophysical Phenomena (astro-ph.HE)
- Reason: Directly addresses JWST AGN at high redshift, supermassive black hole growth, and the overmassive black hole problem, using cosmological simulations and mock JWST observations.

Short digest:
Can early JWST AGN be explained by standard black hole growth models? The AESOPICA simulations show that efficient accretion can assemble the observed population from any seed mass, and broad-line selection naturally produces overmassive AGN. This matters because it provides a physical framework to interpret JWST AGN demographics and distinguish seeding channels.
